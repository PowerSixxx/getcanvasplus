Changelog

What’s new in Canvas+

Every version of the Canvas+ Chrome extension, newest first. Chrome updates it for you automatically.

中文

  1. 1.4.4

    Latest2 changes
    • ImprovedThe red number on the toolbar icon now counts the same thing as the floating Canvas+ button and the Mac app: what’s due in the next 48 hours.
    • NewThe version number is shown at the bottom of Appearance. Click it to open this changelog.

  2. 1.4.3

    5 changes
    • NewWAM (weighted average mark) as an alternative to GPA, with presets for Australia (HD / D / CR / P), Melbourne (H1 / H2A…) and the UK (1st / 2:1…). Type a mark to see “what if”.
    • ImprovedWith WAM, the final calculator aims for the top band by default.
    • NewCustomize grading scale from Appearance too.
    • NewCanvas+ for Mac: a download card at the bottom of Appearance (on Macs).
    • ImprovedRounded, pill-shaped switches.

  3. 1.4.2

    6 changes
    • NewHighlights and notes in the Canvas+ reader: 4 colours, notes, and box-select for scans, pictures and formulas. A “Notes” tab lists them by page.
    • NewDownload slides + notes: the original PDF with your highlights and notes written in.
    • NewMy notes (AI Course tab): every highlight, by course and file.
    • NewWeekly workload: the next 4 weeks as coloured blocks above the deadline list. Tap a week to see what’s due.
    • ImprovedCleaner “Customize menu” for the course menu, with an “Only here” switch.
    • ImprovedRounded pill style everywhere and a smoother GPA ring.

  4. 1.4.1

    2 changes
    • NewCanvas+ reader (Beta): open any PDF on Canvas full-window, zoom from 25% to 800% and stay sharp, jump by section. “Reader” buttons appear next to PDF links.
    • NewResize file previews from the corner, right edge or bottom (switch in Appearance).

  5. 1.4.0

    4 changes
    • NewNew Canvas+ logo (the C+ mark) and Ohio State scarlet everywhere, including the toolbar icon and e-mails.
    • ImprovedCourse Q&A answers appear as they are written.
    • ImprovedLoading placeholders in the shape of the real content, then a smooth reveal.
    • ImprovedA liquid-glass lens on the tab bar and switches, plus layout polish throughout.

  6. 1.3.2

    2 changes
    • NewFilter deadlines by type: assignments, quizzes, discussions, events. The badge and reminders follow the filter.
    • FixedEvents that have ended are no longer shown as overdue (“Ended” / “In progress”).

  7. 1.3.1

    2 changes
    • NewA page for each course: its grade, and its Canvas+ AI tools (Q&A, review packs, mistake book) in one place.
    • ImprovedSmaller, faster package.

  8. 1.3.0

    2 changes
    • NewToolbar popup: click the Canvas+ icon in Chrome’s toolbar to see what’s due next, without opening Canvas. The red number counts what’s due soon.
    • ImprovedYour membership’s name (Plus / Pro) is shown throughout.

  9. 1.2.8

    2 changes
    • NewCanvas+ memberships are here: Plus and Pro give you more AI uses every day.
    • ImprovedShows when your daily AI uses reset; website link at the bottom of Appearance; support e-mail moved to getcanvasplus.com.

  10. 1.2.7

    1 change
    • FixedBug fixes and stability improvements.

  11. 1.2.6

    4 changes
    • NewLecture notes for Zoom recordings: chapters with timestamps (click to jump), key points, and anything said about exams.
    • NewHide the course-menu items and far-left menu icons you never use.
    • New“Copy details for the developer” to make bug reports easier.
    • ImprovedA free Canvas+ account is now needed to start (no password: Google or an e-mail code).

  12. 1.2.5

    1 change
    • ImprovedA reminder to follow your course’s rules on AI use; clearer daily AI limits.

  13. 1.2.4

    1 change
    • FixedBug fixes.

  14. 1.2.3

    4 changes
    • NewSyllabi on Simple Syllabus can be read and summarized too.
    • NewRewrite a syllabus summary with one click.
    • New“Update now” when a new version of Canvas+ is ready.
    • NewPlus and Pro plans announced.

  15. 1.2.2

    3 changes
    • NewGrade breakdown as a pie chart (solid = graded, striped = not yet).
    • ImprovedExam dates are also found in the course’s quizzes and assignments in Canvas, not only the syllabus.
    • NewChange your activation code.

  16. 1.2.1

    2 changes
    • NewCalendar view for deadlines.
    • NewGrade trend: how each course grade got where it is.

  17. 1.2.0

    10 changes
    • NewExam review pack: an outline, practice questions and flashcards from the slides your exam covers.
    • NewMistake book: the practice questions you get wrong, ready to review again (with Anki export).
    • NewCumulative GPA, with your own grading scale.
    • NewClass standing: an estimate of where you are in the class.
    • NewDownload all of a course’s files as one .zip, one folder per module.
    • NewAll my exams, read from every syllabus, ready for your calendar.
    • NewMissing work: only what’s still missing this term.
    • NewNotifications when a deadline moves, and before exams.
    • NewFeedback form: send bugs and ideas straight to the developer.
    • ImprovedDrag to make the panel bigger.

  18. 1.1.0

    6 changes
    • NewCanvas+ AI: a step-by-step guide for any assignment, syllabus summaries, and course Q&A answered from the course itself.
    • NewWeekly digest: new work, deadlines, grade changes and announcements for the week.
    • NewE-mail writer: drafts a message to your professor or TA and fills it into Canvas Inbox.
    • NewNotifications for new grades and announcements.
    • NewYour own background picture for Canvas.
    • NewCanvas+ account: sign in with Google or an e-mail code, no password.

  19. 1.0.1

    1 change
    • FixedSmall fixes.

  20. 1.0.0

    6 changes
    • NewFirst release on the Chrome Web Store.
    • NewEvery deadline in one list, with how much of your grade each one is worth. Mark things done right there.
    • NewWhat do I need on the final? A final-grade calculator that handles weights and extra credit.
    • NewDark mode, course colours and one-click palettes; hide the parts of Canvas you never use.
    • NewSearch everything with ⌘K / Ctrl+K.
    • NewDeadline reminders (24 h and 2 h before) and calendar export (.ics).
