Changelog

What’s new in Canvas+

Every version of the Canvas+ Chrome extension, newest first. Chrome updates it for you automatically.

  1. 1.6.0

    Latest21 changes
    • NewAdjust any Canvas+ theme: tap the pencil on a theme to change its colour, background pattern or font, or pick which of its 8 pictures each course gets. “Back to how it came” undoes it all.
    • ImprovedTheme changes are on the moment you tap — no more “Save and use” button. This goes for your own themes too.
    • NewInvite friends (under your avatar): your own invite link. When a friend who has never used Canvas+ signs up through it, you each get 1 month of Pro (up to 12 months for you). See who joined and how many months you've earned.
    • NewDaily streak: above your deadlines, how many days in a row you've opened Canvas with Canvas+, your best and total days, and this week at a glance. Share it as a picture to show off.
    • NewShare a theme: a picture card with a QR code for Xiaohongshu, Instagram or a group chat. Friends scan it, and with Canvas+ installed the theme opens right in their own Canvas; without it, they're shown where to get Canvas+.
    • NewYour own themes: choose which picture each course gets (for example planes for CSE 2231, a cat for Biology), or leave it on Auto and the pictures take turns.
    • ImprovedEvery Canvas+ theme now has 8 different course-card pictures (was 5), including new animated scenes for Kitten Play (chasing a butterfly, sunbathing, peeking out of a box) and Puppy Play (rainy-day splashing, a birthday party, frisbee at the beach). With more than 8 courses, the pictures come back mirrored.
    • ImprovedThemes now colour the far-left Canvas menu and the page text, lines, buttons and fields too.
    • NewTheme market: a big “theme studio” window (Appearance › Theme market). Browse themes other students made — search, sort by popular or newest, filter by tags — and try one on: the preview shows your own Dashboard with it. “Use” puts it on without taking one of your 3 own themes; adjust it like any theme, or put it back the way it is in the market. You can like and report themes.
    • NewPublish your own theme to the market (My themes › Publish). Every theme is reviewed by hand and goes public only after it’s approved; you can see “In review / Approved / Not approved” with the reason.
    • NewA nickname for your Canvas+ account (My account). Nicknames are unique and can be changed once every 90 days. Other students see only your nickname, never your e-mail. Only the themes you publish are uploaded; everything else stays on your computer.
    • NewFeatured themes: the best themes in the market get a ★ Featured badge, picked by Canvas+. If your theme is picked, you get a month of Pro (up to 3 months).
    • NewTop creators: a row of the students whose themes are liked and used most, and a page for each creator with all of their themes. Type @ and a nickname to find a creator; the list filters as you type.
    • NewProfile picture: add your own picture in My account. It's shown next to your nickname in the theme market; without one you get your first letter on Canvas+ red.
    • ImprovedEvery account now starts with a nickname (like Canvas+4821). Change it to your own any time — the first change doesn't count toward the 90-day limit.
    • NewReward details (under your avatar): every month of Pro you've earned and where it came from — friends who joined, featured themes, event codes — and when it ends.
    • ImprovedEvent activation codes start counting from the day you redeem them, and can be added on top of Pro you already have from rewards.
    • ImprovedThe streak share picture shows your @nickname, so friends know whose it is.
    • ImprovedFonts: four up front (Theme's own, Canvas, KuaiLe, Geometric); the rest are under “More fonts”.
    • ImprovedGoing back from a theme in the market takes you to where you were in the list, not the top.
    • NewAfter a week with Canvas+, a small card asks how you like it: love it and you can rate it on the Chrome Web Store; not so much and you can tell us what to fix. It shows at most 3 times.

  2. 1.5.0

    12 changes
    • NewMake your own themes (up to 3): pick your own pictures for the course cards, a colour, a background pattern and a font. Export it as a file to share with friends — they import it in one tap. Your pictures stay on your computer.
    • NewThemes: 26 original looks in Appearance — City Lights, Open Sea, Forest, Cat Paws, Burger & Fries and more, plus 3 animated ones where the course-card pictures move: Kitten Play and Puppy Play (a different scene on each card — reading, exploring, fishing, flying a kite, digging, listening to music…) and Aquarium. One tap changes the course-card art, the page background, Canvas's colours and the font. Five are shown up front; tap “Show all themes” for the rest.
    • ImprovedThe Canvas+ panel, the search box and the toolbar popup take on the theme's soft colour, faint pattern and font too; the popup also follows your light / dark choice. Themes are light looks: picking one switches dark mode off, and picking dark mode goes back to Classic.
    • ImprovedWith a theme on, the cards, buttons and pills inside the panel use the theme's colours too, so nothing stays bright white.
    • ImprovedSwitching pages no longer flashes white: the theme's background is there from the first moment the next page appears.
    • NewHover over a course's grade trend to see the score on each grading day.
    • ImprovedA shorter first-run tour (6 cards instead of 10), now with a stop for themes. If you already took the tour, you'll just see the themes card once.
    • ImprovedYour own background picture now works like a theme's wallpaper: it sits behind Canvas without changing its layout, menu colours or fonts, and a “Soften picture” slider keeps text easy to read.
    • ImprovedDrop-down menus in the panel (language, grades, adding a course…) are redrawn to match the panel, dark mode and your theme.
    • NewPick your own font for Canvas in Appearance: rounded, serif, handwritten, monospace and more — every font the themes use. It works with any theme, or on its own.
    • NewNew font: KuaiLe (ZCOOL KuaiLe), a playful rounded font for Chinese and English, built into Canvas+ so it works on any computer.
    • NewYour current score in the top-left corner of every course card on the Dashboard, with the course's own grade (A, HD, H1…) or the scale you set in Final calc — just the percentage if there isn't one. Turn it off in Appearance if you'd rather not show it.

  3. 1.4.4

    2 changes

    • ImprovedWith WAM, the final calculator aims for the top band by default.
    • NewCustomize grading scale from Appearance too.
    • NewCanvas+ for Mac: a download card at the bottom of Appearance (on Macs).
    • ImprovedRounded, pill-shaped switches.
