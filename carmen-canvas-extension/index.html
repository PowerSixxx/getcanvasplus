The Carmen Canvas extension, made by a Buckeye.

Canvas+ works on Carmen out of the box — no setup, no school code. Built by an Ohio State student who wanted Carmen to show what’s due, what’s worth what, and what you need on the final.

How it works

  1. Add Canvas+ to Chrome

    Free. Works on Chrome and Edge.

  2. Open Carmen

    Go to carmen.osu.edu and sign in as usual — Canvas+ never asks for your password.

  3. Click the Canvas+ tab

    It’s on the right edge of every Carmen page (or press ⌘K / Ctrl+K).

Questions

Does Canvas+ work on Carmen?

Yes. Carmen is Ohio State’s Canvas, and Canvas+ works on it right away.

Is it safe to use with my OSU account?

Canvas+ reads Carmen with your own session in your browser. It never sees your password or Duo, never submits work and never changes grades.

Is Canvas+ made by Ohio State?

No. Canvas+ is an independent project by an Ohio State student, not affiliated with or endorsed by The Ohio State University.
