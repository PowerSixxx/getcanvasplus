Canvas dark mode

A real dark mode for Canvas.

One switch turns Canvas dark — easy on the eyes for late-night studying. Canvas+ also lets you pick course colours, add your own background photo and tidy up the menus.

How it works

  1. Add Canvas+ to Chrome

    Free. Works on Chrome and Edge.

  2. Open Appearance

    Click the Canvas+ tab on the right edge of any Canvas page (or press ⌘K / Ctrl+K) and choose Appearance.

  3. Turn on Dark mode

    Flip the switch. Canvas stays dark on every page until you turn it off.

What you get

Every Canvas page

Dashboard, courses, assignments, modules and discussions all go dark.

Course colours

Give each course its own colour, or use solid-colour course cards instead of images.

Your own background

Drop in a photo to use as the page background.

A tidier Canvas

Hide the course-menu items and left-menu icons you never use, like People or Chat.

Questions

Does Canvas have a dark mode?

Canvas in the browser has no built-in dark mode. Canvas+ adds one, for every Canvas page.

Is it free?

Yes. Dark mode, course colours and themes are free forever.

Does it change anything for my instructor?

No. It only changes how Canvas looks in your own browser.
