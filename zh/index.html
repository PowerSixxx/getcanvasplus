FAQ

Questions, answered.常见问题

Is Canvas+ really free?Canvas+ 真的免费吗？
Yes. Deadlines, the calendar, the final calculator, GPA, grade alerts, themes, the mistake book and file downloads are free forever, and every AI feature is included with 3 uses a day. Plus and Pro only add more AI uses.是的。作业倒计时、日历、期末计算器、GPA、出分提醒、界面美化、错题本和课件打包永久免费，所有 AI 功能也都能用，每天 3 次。Plus 和 Pro 只是增加 AI 次数。
Will it do my homework for me?它会替我写作业吗？
No. Canvas+ AI explains what an assignment asks, how to approach it and where people lose points — it never writes the answers. Whether to use AI at all is up to you and each course’s rules.不会。Canvas+ AI 告诉你作业要求什么、怎么入手、哪里容易扣分，但不会替你写答案。是否使用 AI 由你根据每门课的规定决定。
Is it safe? What does it change in Canvas?安全吗？会改动我的 Canvas 吗？
Canvas+ runs in your browser and reads Canvas with your own login, the same pages you can already see. It never submits work or changes grades; the only thing it writes back is a to-do you tick off yourself (synced with Canvas To Do).Canvas+ 在你的浏览器里运行，用你自己的登录读取你本来就能看到的页面。它不会提交作业或改动成绩，唯一会写回 Canvas 的是你自己打勾完成的待办（和 Canvas 待办同步）。
Does it work at my school?我的学校能用吗？
If your school uses Canvas, yes — Ohio State’s Carmen and any school on instructure.com work right away. If your school has its own Canvas address, open the panel there once and turn it on.只要学校用 Canvas 就能用：OSU 的 Carmen 和所有 instructure.com 的学校装好就能用；如果学校的 Canvas 是自己的网址，在那里打开一次面板并开启即可。
Why do I need to sign in?为什么要登录？
A free Canvas+ account (Google, or a 6-digit code to your e-mail — no password) keeps AI use fair and lets a plan follow you to any computer. It stores only your e-mail and plan.一个免费的 Canvas+ 账号（Google 或邮箱 6 位验证码，不用密码）用来公平分配 AI 次数，也让会员能跟着你换电脑。账号只保存邮箱和会员状态。
Which browsers?支持哪些浏览器？
Chrome and Microsoft Edge on computers (Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, Linux). On a Mac you can also get the free Canvas+ for Mac app with a menu bar panel and desktop widgets.电脑上的 Chrome 和 Microsoft Edge（Windows、macOS、ChromeOS、Linux）。Mac 用户还可以下载免费的 Canvas+ Mac 版，带菜单栏面板和桌面小组件。
I have the extension. Why get the Mac app?已经装了插件，为什么还要下载 Mac 版？
The extension only shows up while Canvas is open in Chrome. The Mac app puts the same information on your desktop with widgets: your next deadline, the week ahead and your grades are always in view, and you can tick items off right in the widget — no browser to open. It also adds a menu bar panel, a full app window and reminders that arrive even when Chrome is closed. It’s free, uses the same rules and grade math as the extension, and you can use both at once.
